The 6 Customer Personas

Each persona is built from the real behavioral averages of its members — no assumptions, no guesswork.
The "So What?" framework answers the only question that matters to the Marketing Director: what do we actually do with this?

Note on cluster merging: The model initially produced 10 groups. Four groups (clusters 2, 3, 6, 8) shared overlapping behavioral profiles (low frequency, high inactivity, low average spend) and were consolidated into a single Dormant Potential segment. Two mid-tier groups (clusters 0, 9) with near-identical KPIs were merged into Rising Stars. The result is 6 genuinely distinct segments — each requiring a different marketing lever.

Technical Appendix

Methodology details for the data science team — not required for the executive presentation

End-to-end pipeline (CRISP-DM)

7 phases: Data Preparation → EDA → Feature Engineering → Dimensionality Reduction → Clustering → Profiling → Financial Modeling. Full pipeline reproducible from the main notebook.

Feature Engineering (32 features)

Extended RFM: recency, frequency, monetary, avg_basket.
Discount propensity: avg_discount_pct, pct_items_discounted.
Email engagement: nl_open_rate, nl_click_rate, composite engagement_score.
Product breadth: n_categories, pct per category.
Returns: return_rate. Demographics: age_years, tenure_days.

Why UMAP + GMM?

K-Means rejected: assumes spherical, equal-size clusters — violated by behavioral data.
DBSCAN/OPTICS rejected: "hairball problem" — uniform density, one mega-cluster.
UMAP: non-linear projection preserving local topology → behavioral sub-groups become separable.
GMM: ellipsoidal clusters + soft assignment (each customer gets a confidence score).

K selection: BIC + business cap

BIC computed for K=3 to K=10. On continuous behavioral data, BIC may decrease monotonically — pure minimisation returned K=10 with duplicate segment profiles. Final K=6 selected via: (1) BIC elbow at 20% of first marginal drop, (2) operational cap K≤6 — beyond 6 segments, distinct marketing campaigns become unmanageable (industry benchmark).

Cluster -1 (Statistical Outlier)

The model automatically isolated 1 customer as a statistical outlier via DBSCAN — representing 0.005% of the base. This is not a targetable segment. Likely: a test account, B2B reseller, or data entry anomaly. Excluded from all strategic analysis and ROI calculations.
